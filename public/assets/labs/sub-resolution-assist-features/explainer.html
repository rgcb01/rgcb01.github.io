Simulated, not measured. Model: 1D scalar Abbe imaging of a thin bright-field binary mask (chrome on glass), dry ArF 193 nm, NA 0.85, annular illumination sigma 0.55-0.85 sampled at 601 point-symmetric source points, exact scalar defocus; positive-tone resist modelled as a constant threshold after a 12 nm Gaussian blur; dose in units of the dose to clear, threshold = 1/dose. Synthetic layout: one isolated 90 nm line with 0 or 2 assist bars per side (110 nm from the line, 200 nm bar pitch, width 0-60 nm in exact 5 nm steps), dense 90 nm lines at 200 nm pitch for comparison. Process window: CD within +-10 %, 5 % exposure latitude, DOF by the exposure-defocus method; defect-free = nothing but the line prints; safe = every assist at least 10 % above threshold (educational heuristic). The drawing is interpolated between exact 5 nm width steps while a width animates; every number shown is the exact step. Stack, plates and light are an own schematic design. Educational model, not industrial assist placement: no 2D effects, polarization, mask 3D, resist calibration or mask error.
